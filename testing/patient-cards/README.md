# Patient cards for role-players

One card per drill patient. Give the role-player only their own card. The EMT never sees it.

Rules for every role-player:

- Never speak English (the ASL card: never speak at all).
- Native speakers: answer naturally in your language, with the facts on the card.
- Scripted role-players: say or type the lines on the card. In demo mode, type them exactly.
- Do not volunteer facts the EMT has not asked about, unless the card says to.
- Act it: point to where it hurts, look worried. Do not help the EMT.
- On the confirm screen, press Yes only if what it shows is right. If it is wrong, press No: that is part of the test.
