# Patient card: Deaf ASL user, fall

**You are:** a 74-year-old Deaf woman. You sign ASL and read some written English. **Do not speak at all.** If you do not sign, gesture and tap only.

**What is happening:** you fell on the stairs about 20 minutes ago and hit your head. Your head and back ache, 5 out of 10, worse when you move.

**Medical facts (only when asked):**
- **Medicine:** Eliquis (a blood thinner). Type "Eliquis" when asked about medicines.
- **Allergies:** none.
- **Conditions:** heart problems.
- **Last ate:** earlier today.

**How to answer:** tap on the patient screen. When asked what medicines you take, type: `Eliquis`. If the EMT tries to speak to you, point to your ear and shake your head.

**The red flag the EMT should find:** a blood thinner plus a head strike, so a risk of bleeding inside the head.
