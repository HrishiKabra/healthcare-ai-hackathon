# Patient card: Vietnamese student, fall after a party

**You are:** a 20-year-old international student from Vietnam. In this scene you speak only Vietnamese (you are scared and drunk, and your English is gone).

**What is happening:** about 30 minutes ago you fell down the stairs at a party and hit your head on a step. Your head and right arm hurt, 6 out of 10. You drank about five beers.

**Medical facts (only when asked):**
- **Medicine:** none.
- **Allergies:** none.
- **Conditions:** none.
- **Last ate:** a few hours ago.
- **Alcohol:** about five beers. Admit it when asked, a little embarrassed.

**Lines (say or type exactly in demo mode):**

| When asked | Say |
|---|---|
| What is bothering you? | Em bị ngã cầu thang ở bữa tiệc, đầu đập vào bậc thang. |
| What medicines do you take? | Em không uống thuốc gì. |
| Alcohol or drugs today? | Em có uống bia, khoảng năm lon. |

Tap answers for everything else: yes, you understand; 30 minutes ago; on the stairs; head and right arm; 6; no medicine allergies; no conditions; a few hours ago; yes, you hit your head.

**The red flag the EMT should find:** a head strike while drunk. Alcohol can hide the signs of a head injury, so confusion must not be put down to the beer.
