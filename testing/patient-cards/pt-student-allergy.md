# Patient card: Brazilian student, allergic reaction

**You are:** a 19-year-old student from Brazil. In this scene you speak only Portuguese (you are panicking and struggling to breathe).

**What is happening:** about 15 minutes ago you ate something at the dining hall that had peanuts. You are allergic to peanuts. Your throat feels like it is closing, your lips are swelling, and you cannot breathe well.

**Medical facts (only when asked):**
- **Allergy:** peanuts. No medicine allergies.
- **Medicine:** none. You own an epinephrine pen, but it is in your dorm room.
- **Conditions:** asthma.
- **Last ate:** just now, at the dining hall.

| What medicines do you take? | Não tomo nenhum remédio. |
| Anything else? | Tenho uma caneta de adrenalina, mas ficou no meu quarto. |

Tap answers for everything else: yes, you understand; 15 minutes ago; eating; yes, allergic; asthma; minutes ago.

**The red flag the EMT should find:** a known allergy plus trouble breathing, so possible anaphylaxis. Watch the airway and consider epinephrine.
