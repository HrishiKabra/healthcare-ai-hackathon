## Email (tulane-ems@tulane.edu)

Subject: 30 minutes of EMT feedback on a student hackathon prototype

Hi Tulane EMS,

We are a student team in the Freeman AI Innovation Challenge. We built FirstWords, a prototype that helps an EMT run the standard field assessment with a patient who does not speak English, is Deaf, or cannot speak. The patient answers in their own language, in ASL, or on a picture board. The tool builds a chart, flags red flags with fixed rules, and produces an English handoff that includes the patient's communication needs.

Tulane's international community comes largely from India, Vietnam, China, Mexico, and Brazil, and TEMS is often first on scene before NOEMS, so we think your crews are the right first users to learn from.

Could two to six members give us about 30 minutes before Monday, October 26 at 3 PM? Each EMT runs two role-played patients, one with FirstWords and one their usual way, and fills in a short survey. There are no real patients, and we record only timings and survey answers under a code, not names. This is a test of the tool, not of the EMTs, and it is not an official TEMS activity.
